# Legal Documents for KidsMath App Store Submission

This directory contains the legal documents required for submitting KidsMath to Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

## Documents Included

### 1. PRIVACY_POLICY.md
- Complete privacy policy for app store submission
- COPPA compliant for children's apps
- GDPR compliant for international users
- Covers all data collection and usage practices

### 2. TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md
- Complete terms of service agreement
- Educational app specific clauses
- Parental responsibility sections
- Liability limitations and dispute resolution

### 3. HTML Files (Ready for Web Hosting)
- **privacy-policy.html** - Styled HTML version of privacy policy
- **terms-of-service.html** - Styled HTML version of terms of service
- **legal-documents.html** - Index page with links to both documents
- Mobile-responsive design with professional styling
- Direct URLs for app store submission

### 4. In-App Legal Text
- Simplified versions are implemented in the login screen
- Users can view summaries before agreeing to terms
- Full documents should be hosted on your website

## Before App Store Submission

### Required Updates
1. **Replace placeholder contact information:**
   - Update `privacy@kidsmath.app` with your actual email
   - Update `support@kidsmath.app` with your actual support email
   - Add your business address in both documents
   - Update jurisdiction information in Terms of Service

2. **Host documents online:**
   - Upload the HTML files to your website
   - Recommended URLs:
     - `https://yourdomain.com/privacy-policy.html`
     - `https://yourdomain.com/terms-of-service.html`
     - `https://yourdomain.com/legal-documents.html` (optional index)
   - Ensure they are accessible via direct URLs
   - App stores require web-accessible privacy policies
   - Test all links before app store submission

### App Store Requirements

#### Google Play Store
- Privacy Policy URL is required in Play Console
- Must comply with Families Policy for children's apps
- Data safety section must match privacy policy
- Target age group must be specified

#### Apple App Store
- Privacy Policy URL required in App Store Connect
- App privacy details must be completed
- Age rating must reflect target audience (4+)
- Parental gate may be required for certain features

### Data Collection Summary for App Stores

**Data Collected:**
- Nickname (stored locally)
- Game progress and scores (stored locally)
- Anonymous usage statistics (for app improvement)

**Data NOT Collected:**
- Real names or personal identifiers
- Location data
- Contact information
- Photos or camera access
- Microphone access

**Third-Party Services:**
- None (all audio files are bundled with app)
- No analytics services
- No advertising networks
- No social media integration
