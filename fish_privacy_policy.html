FirstMathFish Privacy Policy

Good2Good Educational Technology

Effective Date: August 12, 2024

1. Introduction

Good2Good ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our FirstMathFish application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information to provide you with the best educational experience:

Personal Information:

  • Nickname (for game identification)
  • Game progress and scores
  • User preferences

Non-Personal Information:

  • Device information (device type, operating system)
  • App usage data (features used, time spent)
  • Performance data (app crashes, errors)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide personalized educational content
  • Track learning progress and achievements
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Analyze app performance and fix issues
  • Provide customer support when needed

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage:

All your data is stored locally on your device using secure storage methods. We do not transmit your personal information to external servers.

Data Protection:

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal requirements
  • To protect our rights and safety

6. Children's Privacy

FirstMathFish is designed for children and families. We are committed to protecting children's privacy:

  • We collect minimal information necessary for app functionality
  • No personal information is shared with third parties
  • Parents have full control over their children's data
  • All data is stored locally on the device

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your stored data
  • Delete your data at any time
  • Opt out of data collection (though this may limit app functionality)
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

8. Data Retention

Your data is stored locally on your device and will be retained as long as you use the app. You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Clearing app data in device settings
  • Uninstalling the app
  • Using the in-app data deletion feature

9. Third-Party Services

FirstMathFish may use third-party services for:

  • Analytics (anonymous usage data only)
  • App store distribution
  • Customer support

These services are bound by their own privacy policies and do not have access to your personal information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy in the app
  • Updating the app store listing
  • Sending in-app notifications

11. Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • App Store privacy requirements
  • Google Play Store privacy requirements
