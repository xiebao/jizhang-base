KidsMath Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Updated: January 1, 2025
Important: KidsMath is designed for children aged 4 and above. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and complying with all applicable privacy laws, including COPPA and GDPR.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how KidsMath ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application KidsMath (the "App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and complying with applicable privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Nickname: Users can create a nickname for their profile, which is stored locally on the device.
  • Game Progress: We collect and store game scores, difficulty levels completed, and progress data locally on the device.

Non-Personal Information

  • Device Information: We may collect non-personal information about your device, including device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience, such as which levels are played most frequently.

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • To track and display user progress and achievements
  • To improve the App's educational content and user experience
  • To ensure the App operates correctly on your device

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All personal data (nickname and game progress) is stored locally on your device
  • No personal information is transmitted to external servers
  • Data remains on your device and is not shared with third parties

Security Measures

  • We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
  • Data is protected using industry-standard encryption methods
  • Access to any data is restricted and controlled

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

KidsMath is designed for children aged 4 and above. We are committed to protecting children's privacy:

  • We do not collect personal information from children without parental consent
  • We do not share children's personal information with third parties
  • We do not use children's personal information for marketing purposes
  • Parents can request deletion of their child's data at any time

Parental Rights

Parents have the right to:

  • Review their child's personal information
  • Request deletion of their child's personal information
  • Refuse to allow further collection of their child's personal information

6. Third-Party Services

Audio Content

The App includes audio files (background music and sound effects) that are stored locally within the app bundle. No external audio services are used.

No Analytics or Advertising

  • We do not use third-party analytics services
  • We do not display advertisements
  • We do not use tracking technologies or cookies

7. Data Retention

  • User data (nickname and game progress) is retained until the user uninstalls the app
  • Users can delete their data at any time by clearing the app's data through device settings
  • No data is retained on external servers

8. International Data Transfers

Since all data is stored locally on the user's device, no international data transfers occur.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your personal data
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal data

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • For significant changes, we may provide additional notice through the App

11. Data Collection Summary for App Stores

Data Collected

  • Contact Info: None collected
  • Identifiers: None collected
  • Usage Data: Anonymous usage statistics (not linked to user identity)
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and performance data (not linked to user identity)

Data Use Summary

  • App Functionality: Nickname and game progress for core app features
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data for app improvement
  • Product Personalization: Game progress to customize user experience

Data Sharing Summary

  • Third Parties: No data is shared with third parties
  • Service Providers: No third-party service providers have access to user data

12. Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Google Play Developer Policy
  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines
