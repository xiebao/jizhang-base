# Privacy Policy for XMoneyNote

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how XMoneyNote ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application XMoneyNote (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your financial data.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Username**: Users create a username for account identification, stored locally on the device
- **Email Address**: Used for account registration and verification, stored locally on the device
- **Password**: Encrypted and stored locally for authentication purposes
- **Financial Data**: Transaction records, categories, amounts, dates, and notes are stored locally on the device

### Non-Personal Information
- **Device Information**: We may collect non-personal information about your device, including device type, operating system version, and app version
- **Usage Data**: We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience

## How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- To provide and maintain the App's core functionality
- To track and display your financial transactions and statistics
- To generate charts and reports for your financial analysis
- To improve the App's user experience and features
- To ensure the App operates correctly on your device

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- All personal and financial data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database
- No personal or financial information is transmitted to external servers
- Data remains on your device and is not shared with third parties
- Passwords are encrypted using SHA-256 hashing before storage

### Security Measures
- We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Data is protected using industry-standard encryption methods
- Access to any data is restricted and controlled
- All financial transactions are stored securely on your device

## Data Sharing

### No Third-Party Sharing
- We do not share your personal or financial information with third parties
- We do not sell, trade, or rent your information to others
- We do not use your data for marketing purposes
- We do not display advertisements

### Service Providers
- We do not use third-party service providers that have access to your data
- All data processing occurs locally on your device

## Data Retention

- User data (username, email, transactions) is retained until the user uninstalls the app
- Users can delete their data at any time by clearing the app's data through device settings
- No data is retained on external servers
- Users can export their data before uninstalling the app

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:
- **Access**: Request access to your personal data
- **Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your personal data
- **Portability**: Request transfer of your personal data
- **Objection**: Object to processing of your personal data

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.
